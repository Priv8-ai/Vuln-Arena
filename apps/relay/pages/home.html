What the console is for

Three jobs, kept in the order operators actually do them.

A session, then the call

Login returns a bearer token. Notes, profile, and GraphQL all expect that header.

Notes that stay with an owner

Each note records who it belongs to. Fetch by id when a service needs the body.

Health before you page anyone

GET /health is the quiet check. Operators can ask the same route for a longer view.
