A collective of principals. No bench behind the door.

The person who takes the first meeting does the work. We hire slowly, in Toronto and Chicago, and we do not staff an engagement with people the client has not met.

How we take work

A conversation first, then a written scope no longer than four pages. If we are the wrong room, we say so before a contract.

Who sits in the room

Operators who have run a P&L, a newsroom, or a public institution. The collective is the list of those people, not a brand laid over contractors.
