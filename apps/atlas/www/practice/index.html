Three practices. We do not invent a fourth for the proposal.

Most of the work is one of these. If it is not, we will say so in the first meeting.

Operating review

Four weeks inside the company. We read the plans that already exist, sit with the people who run the week, and name the one decision that is being postponed. Kindling Bank used this to retire two of three branch plans.

Market entry

A season, not a workshop. We talk to the buyers and the people who already do the work in that place. Northwind’s corridor study came from dispatchers, not from a model.

Successor planning

Ten days when one person has been the institution. The memorandum is short enough for a board to read in a sitting, and it names the work, not a personality.
