A floor, a transcript, and a desk.

Signalwatch is the line a desk speaks on during the session, plus the place those words go when someone needs them reviewed.

On the floor

The channel is a WebSocket at /ws. Each note is sent to every open connection and appended to the transcript.

You do not pick a room. This host is the room.

On the desk

Report abuse files a body with the mod bot. The mod bot opens the queue and shows each report in arrival order.

History remains the JSON transcript if you need the tape without the socket.

What a seat includes

  • A live composer on the home floor
  • A report form that returns a JSON receipt
  • A moderation queue for the bot
  • A published status page for the shift lead
