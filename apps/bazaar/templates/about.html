The house

A courtyard, a desk, and a short list of lots.

Morrow Market started as a Saturday table for makers who were tired of shipping goods into a void. The table became a room. The room still closes when the floor is full.

We know the bench

A lot does not go up because a photo looked warm. Someone at the house has held it, or knows the person who made it.

Prices stay on the tag

What you see on the card is what the desk expects. Quantity is yours. The tag is the maker’s.

Notes stay with the lot

Buyers leave a line. The next collector reads it. We do not sand the comments off to look newer.

Visit

Courtyard room, Harbour Market. The floor is open Wednesday to Sunday. Monday is for makers bringing work in, not for browsing.

If you are buying from elsewhere, the same lots are on this desk, with the same tags. Orders are read by a person before anything is wrapped.
