Field note · Copper

The week a kettle actually takes

A copper kettle looks quick in a photograph. The bench version takes a week of annealing, a day of refusing the first handle, and a last hour where the maker decides the spout is finished. We list it when that hour is over, not when the picture is sharp.

The lot on the floor now is that kind of object. The tag names the seller. The price is the one agreed before it left the bench.

House rule

Why the tag still has a name

A marketplace can hide the maker behind a shop title. We tried that for a month in the early Saturday years. Buyers asked fewer questions and returned more goods. The name went back on the tag.

If you are selling, that is the condition. If you are buying, it is the reason the floor is short. Spice, cloth, and copper are all treated the same way: a person, a price, a sentence.
